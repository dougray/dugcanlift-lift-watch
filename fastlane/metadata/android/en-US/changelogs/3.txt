LIFT Link: pair the watch with LIFT for Android 1.17 or later over Bluetooth with a six-digit code. Then send today's workout and run it on your wrist: the prescription in big digits, last time's numbers, rest that buzzes at zero, left and right sides, supersets, drop sets and rest-pause sets, and your heart rate. Finished sessions go back to the phone with each set's highest heart rate.

Food logging reads better with a screen reader and fits small round screens. LIFT's own icon.
